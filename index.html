Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

Where is Brigade Granada located? ▾

Brigade Granada is located on Whitefield- Kadugodi Road, Sannatammanahalli, Bangalore East (Karnataka – 560067) — close to Kadugodi Metro Station and adjacent to Bearys Global Research Triangle.

What configurations are available at Brigade Granada? ▾

Brigade Granada offers premium luxury residences in 1 BHK (730 sq.ft), 2 BHK (1275 sq.ft), 3 BHK (1540 - 1820 sq.ft), 3.5 BHK (2240 sq.ft), and 4 BHK (2760 sq.ft) configurations.

What is the starting price of Brigade Granada? ▾

The residences start at ₹1.07 Cr* onwards for 1 BHK, ₹1.79 Cr* for 2 BHK, ₹2.19 Cr* for 3 BHK, and ₹3.99 Cr* for 4 BHK layouts. All prices exclude PLC, FRC, and SDR charges.

How many units are in Brigade Granada? ▾

Brigade Granada spans a 40-acre land parcel with 6 clusters in total. The initial launch includes 2 clusters containing 1,500 units in G+48 floor configurations.

Who is the developer of Brigade Granada? ▾

Brigade Granada is developed by Brigade Group, one of India's most trusted real estate developers with 250+ delivered projects.
